Getting coordinates in game

  1. Open the map and zoom all the way in on your mortar. Right-click it and choose Mark coordinates.
  2. The coordinates appear in chat. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C, and paste them into Your mortar.
  3. Do the same on the target and paste into Target.
  4. Middle-click the target on the map to drop a marker. On the mortar, turn until the marker diamond is centered. The compass at the top of the scope should read the heading shown here.
  5. Set the RNG scale on the left of the scope to the distance and fire. C skips the shell camera and R loads another round (up to three).
